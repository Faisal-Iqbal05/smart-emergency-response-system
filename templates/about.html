ABOUT NEIGHBOURAID

Local coordination, with clear expectations

Residents can ask for help and share updates with authorised guardians and community responders. A request is confirmed only after this server saves it.

In a life-threatening situation

Call 112 directly in India. NeighbourAid is a community coordination tool and does not replace official emergency response.

Notifications

This demo records in-app notifications as Simulated. Email, SMS, and push providers are not configured.

Privacy

Exact incident details are restricted to the resident and authorised participants. Keep descriptions brief and share only information needed to coordinate help.
